Russia Kept Commuting. It Stopped Travelling.

In July 2026 crashes on roads between towns fell to 21% below their four-year July average, while crashes inside towns barely moved. The two had tracked each other every July since the measures began.

Source: GIBDD monthly road-safety statistics, stat.gibdd.ru — crashes outside towns and settlements (indicator 498) against crashes inside built-up areas (indicator 144), July 2022 to July 2026. Each series indexed to its own 2022–2025 July average. Compiled by Tochnyi
